Image Filter Service

  1. Open Settings → Apps → Safari → Extensions (on iOS 16–17: Settings → Safari → Extensions).
  2. Turn on Image Filter Service and set All Websites to Allow.
  3. That’s it. Images are checked on your iPhone itself — no server, nothing leaves the phone.

Images stay hidden until they’ve been checked. Allowing all websites matters: many pages load pictures from other sites, and the extension can only check pictures from sites it’s allowed on.
